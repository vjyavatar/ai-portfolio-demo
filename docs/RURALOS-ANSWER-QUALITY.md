# Answer usefulness and scenario audit — 29 September 2026

This is a code/content review of all 42 catalogue workflows and their 76 non-travel purpose paths, with live browser reproduction of the missing-payment PM-KISAN answer. It is not 42 completed provider transactions or rural-user research. Existing rendered tests cover all 38 preparation components in three interface languages.

## What the user complaint established

The farmer result said to inspect a pending requirement, without explaining eKYC versus bank seeding versus land verification versus an apparently credited payment. The same generic pending/blocked paragraph is reused across the other preparation services. The previous navigation release did not close that information gap. More catalogue cards, checklist ticks and test counts are not evidence that a household can resolve its case.

## Evaluation rule

A useful result should explain the situation, cite an applicable current fact, name the next action and responsible route, say what to keep ready, explain what to do if it fails, and disclose unknown costs/time/eligibility. Count a transaction only after an authorised provider confirms it. Do not count a link click as completion.

## Coverage after this increment

- Three services gain 11 explicit source-backed situation answers, with English/Hindi/Telugu text and matching spoken answers. No free-text guessing. Unknown/cross-service choices produce clarification; invalid regions or stale sources suppress factual answers. Offline answers are labelled cached and external actions disabled. These are limited national guidance paths, not a state eligibility engine.
- Aadhaar retains its earlier source-backed mobile-number aid; other update subtypes remain incomplete.
- The remaining 34 non-travel services provide editorial preparation and official routes, with the gaps below.
- Four travel catalogue services provide planning/manual quote comparison. Sailing is an extra planning mode, not an additional integrated booking service. No booking, government submission or payment service is integrated by this release.

## Scenario answer contract

Each new branch contains an official fact separately from editorial next-action/preparation/follow-up guidance. National scope, chosen region, source publication information, review date (28 September UTC), and review-due date (12 October UTC) are shown. An old-age or widow pension central share is never described as the state total or a personal approval. No Aadhaar, bank, ration-card, OTP or account data is collected. Native-speaker and representative rural-user review are still needed; Hindi/Telugu stay labelled previews.

Sources: PM-KISAN homepage, Know Your Status and official Point of Contact page; Agriculture Ministry PIB release2225989 (10 February2026); Rural Development Ministry PIB release2210378 (1 January2026); ONORC PIB release1847504 (2 August2022) corroborated by the current DFPD distribution-page search excerpt; NFSA portal and public grievance route. The NSAP FAQ and one DFPD rendered content retrieval were unavailable; no fresh rule is attributed to those failed retrievals. No current fee, local office address or payment deadline was invented. Exact links live beside the data in ruralos-src/lib/service-scenarios.ts.

## Next acceptance checks

1. Ask households to explain the selected answer back in their own language and name their next action; measure this without developer prompting.
2. Follow a small number of real cases with consent through the responsible provider, recording actual completion and unresolved causes privately.
3. Prioritise verified certificate, scholarship and property subtypes next; do not expand via generic pending messages.
4. Obtain authorised case/inventory integrations only where permissions exist. A new AI library alone cannot supply missing official records or live prices.


### Entrepreneurship follow-up, 29 September 2026
The business route now has a dedicated seven-programme central-government guide, five purpose filters, specific actions and a private session-only preparation checklist. This supersedes its earlier checklist-only assessment. That leaves 33 other non-travel services largely at preparation depth; four travel workflows remain planning-only. National programme summaries do not establish state entitlements, open allocations or individual loan eligibility. Homepage now includes a 28-second illustrated usage video, chosen-language captions, a spoken transcript and service shortcuts. These changes are not evidence of rural-household comprehension or successfully obtained credit.
