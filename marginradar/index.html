Who is this useful for?

Independent refurbished-laptop dealers, small IT equipment resellers and buyers comparing batches from suppliers. You need a supplier quote and your own resale estimate; MarginRadar does not supply buyers or guarantee demand.

Will it buy stock or make money automatically?

No. Current analysis uses rules and your inputs. It helps you review costs and missing information. Purchasing, supplier verification and resale remain your decisions. Payments and paid plans are not enabled.
