📚 What are Regional Intelligence Reports?
Think of this as your daily briefing from a fund manager's desk. Before institutional investors at Goldman Sachs or Morgan Stanley make any trade, they check 6 things every morning: macro health (are economies growing?), institutional flows (where is the smart money going?), currency risk (will FX eat my returns?), sector rotation (which industries are leading?), EM vs DM positioning (should I overweight India or US?), and geopolitical signals (any black swans?).

🎓 The analogy: Imagine you're a ship captain. Individual stock analysis tells you about your ship (engine, fuel, crew). But these regional reports tell you about the weather, ocean currents, and nearby ships. You wouldn't sail without checking the weather — and you shouldn't invest without checking macro conditions. A great company in a bad market environment can still lose you money.

These reports update every 5 minutes. Each section compares India (IN) vs USA (US) side by side — the same data format used by Morningstar, Seeking Alpha, and institutional research desks, but generated live from 30+ real-time data feeds.
