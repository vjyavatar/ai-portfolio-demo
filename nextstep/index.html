Before you start

Is NextStep free? Do I need an AI subscription?

NextStep is free to use and does not require an AI subscription. It matches your choices to curated service ideas. The optional prompts can be used with an AI tool you already have access to.

Will it find customers or guarantee an income?

No. It helps you define an offer, talk to potential customers, and test whether the work is useful. Customer acquisition, pricing, qualifications, and permission to do the work remain your responsibility.

How do I save and resume my progress?

Choose Download plan to save a JSON file. Later, choose Open saved plan and select that file. Your checklist, notes, and experiment results are restored. There is no automatic saving or synchronization across devices.

What happens to my information?

The planner processes your choices and notes in your browser. There are no app analytics, ad trackers, or AI requests. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection logs. Downloaded files contain your entries; keep them private. Social buttons open third-party services under their own policies.
