How to use this desk — 4 simple steps
  1. Choose: United States (SPY / QQQ) or India (NIFTY / BANKNIFTY), then select a symbol.
  2. Scan: Research starts automatically and repeats every five minutes by default while the tab stays visible.
  3. Decide: The top panel says WAIT, NO TRADE or REVIEW. Open Market Checks and Options Evidence to understand why. Neither a bullish view nor an OI wall is automatically a CE/PE buy.
  4. Protect: Read the invalidation, stop scenario and premium at risk; paper-practice first. Turn on auto-scan (every 5 or 15 minutes) and browser alerts while the tab stays visible.

Options buying is high-risk. No verified contract, stale data, closed session or missing risk evidence means NO TRADE. Scans do not execute broker orders.
