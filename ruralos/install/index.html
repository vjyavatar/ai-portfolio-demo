Keep Saathi on your phone

Open the app from your home screen. Public guidance is free to explore.

Open Saathi

Android

  1. Open Saathi in Chrome.
  2. Open the browser menu (three dots).
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app, if available.
  4. Confirm. Look for Saathi on your home screen.

iPhone

  1. Open Saathi in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. Depending on your iOS version, this may be under more options.
  4. Tap Add.

What works today?

This installs the web app. It is not an App Store or Google Play release. Browser menus and installation availability vary.

Voice requires a supported browser, microphone permission and an available matching language voice. You can always use buttons or text. Hindi and Telugu are review previews.

Previously loaded public guidance can be cached for interrupted connections. Check the source and verification date before acting. Saved family records and reminders open the existing secure app and require internet access. Background reminder notifications are not available.
